Welcome

Service is our spiritual practice

The Adi Shankaracharya Vedic Foundation is a spiritual and cultural initiative inspired by Jagadguru Adi Shankaracharya, whose teachings of Advaita Vedanta remind us that all life is one.

Under the guidance of Yogrishi Acharya Ashutosh Ji Maharaj, a yoga reformer with over 30 years of impact, that idea becomes action: transforming prisons into places of learning, bringing healing to those who cannot afford it, and caring for the land that sustains us.
